A few things that help

Get comfortable in the kitchen.

Cooking is mostly practice. These small habits make recipes easier to follow and food more consistent.

Before you start

Read the recipe through

Check the full ingredient list, cooking time, and tools first. Set everything out so you are not searching while a pan is hot.

Prep

Cut evenly

Try to make vegetables and meat pieces a similar size. They will cook at about the same pace.

Heat

Give the pan time

A properly warmed pan helps food brown. If ingredients are crowded, cook in batches instead.

Taste

Season a little at a time

Add salt, acid, or spice in small amounts, taste, and adjust. It is easier to add more than to take it out.

Food safety

Keep raw meat separate

Wash hands and surfaces after handling raw meat. Use a food thermometer when a recipe gives a safe internal temperature.

Try it out

Make an easy breakfast

Practice whisking, pan heat, and flipping with a simple pancake recipe.
